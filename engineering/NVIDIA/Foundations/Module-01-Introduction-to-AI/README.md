# Overview

Essential AI Knowledge forms the conceptual foundation of the NVIDIA Certified Associate: AI Infrastructure & Operations (NCA-AIIO) certification.

Before understanding GPU architecture, AI infrastructure, networking, and operations, it is important to understand how Artificial Intelligence evolved, how AI models learn, and why NVIDIA GPUs are fundamental to modern AI computing.

This module focuses on the core AI concepts required throughout the remaining certification domains.

## 6. Training vs Inference

Training

- Learning from data
- Model creation
- High computational requirements

Inference

- Using trained models
- Predictions
- Low-latency execution

Understand why GPUs accelerate both.

---

## 7. CPU vs GPU

CPU

- General-purpose processing
- Sequential execution
- Operating systems
- Application control

GPU

- Massive parallel processing
- Matrix operations
- Deep Learning
- AI acceleration

Understand why modern AI depends on GPUs.
